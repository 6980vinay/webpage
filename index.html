Tic Tac Toc

Tic-Tac-Toe is a simple game where two players take turns marking spaces on a 3x3 grid. One player uses X, and the other uses O. The goal is to get three of your marks in a row, either horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
Here’s how it works:
1. The grid has 9 squares (3 rows and 3 columns).
2. Player 1 is X, and Player 2 is O.
3. Players take turns placing their mark in an empty square.
4. The first person to line up three of their marks in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins.
5. If all the squares are filled and no one has three in a row, the game is a tie.
